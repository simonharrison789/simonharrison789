# Hands

Powder blue sky and salmon clouds
A chiffchaff calls its name
The moon looks down, a daisy looks up
The working hands of the Father

A gentle nudge, a quiet voice
A calmness in the clamour

The guiding hands of the Spirit

A crown of thorns. with arms stretched wide
The searing pain of nails
My debt is paid, I am redeemed
The glorious hands of the Saviour
